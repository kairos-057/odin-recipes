Grilled Chicken

Description

Sweet,sticky and grilled to perfection.My go-to chicken, glazed in Jimmy's BBQ sauce and gone in minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 Full chicken
  • 1 to 1 and half cups of Jimmy's BBQ sauce
  • 2 tablesspoons of olive or cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon of salt
  • 1 teaspoon of black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika(for extra color)
  1. Spatchcock: Place the chicken breast-side down on a cutting board Use a sharp knife to cut along both sides of the backbone and remove it. FLip the chicken over and press doen firmly on the breastbone until you hear a crack and the chicken lies completely flat.
  2. Season: Pat the skin dry with paper towels(helps it crisp up). Rub all over with oil, salt, pepper and paprika.
  3. Set up a two-zone firePrepare your braai or gas grill for medium indirect heat with coal banked to one side or hald the burners off.
  4. Grill skin-side up: Place the flattened chicken on the cool side of the grill (indirect heat), skin-side up, with the legs facing the hotter coals. Close the lid and cook for about 35-40 minutes without lifting the lid too often
  5. Baste with Jimmy's BBQ sauce: Once the skin is golden, brush a generous layer of JImmy's BBQ sauce over the chicken. Move it briefly over diret meddium heat(skin-side down for 2-3 minutes, then flip and baste again) for the last 10-15 minutes to caramelize the sauce. Watch closely so the sauce doesn't burn
  6. Check temparature and rest: The chicken is done when the meat themometer inserted into the thickest part of the thigh reads 75-80°C(and the breast reads 74°C), or when the juices run clear. Transfer to a board, tent loosely with foil and rest for 10 minutes before carving.
