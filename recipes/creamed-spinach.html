Creamed Spinach

h2>Description

Silky, rich and comforting. Tender spinach folded into a velvety cream sauce with a hint of garlic. The perfect side for a Sunday roast or a braai

Ingredients

  • 2 bunches of fresh spinach
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced or crushed
  • 2 tablespoons of butter
  • 1 cup fresh cream
  • 1/2 cup grated cheddar or crubbled feta cheese(optional)
  • 1/2 teaspoon of salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon of black pepper
  • Pinch of nutmeg(optional)
  1. Wilt and drain the spinach: Place the wached spinach in a large pot over medium heat with just water clinging to the leaves. Cover and steam for 2-3 minutes until wilted, then drain and squeeze out as much liquid as possible
  2. Saute the onion and garlic: Melt the butter in a pan over medium heat. Fry the chopped onion for 4-5 minutes until soft, then add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
  3. Reduce the cream: Pour the fresh cream into the pan and let it simmer gently for 3-4 minutes,stirring ocassionally until it thickens slightly.
  4. Combine and season: Stir in the squeezed spinach, salt, black pepper, and nutmeg. Simmer on low heat for another 2-3 minutes so the cream coats the leaves.
  5. Finish: \Stir through the grated cheddar or feta right before serving, if using.
